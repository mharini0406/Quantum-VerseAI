What is a Qubit?

A qubit (Quantum Bit) is the basic unit of information in a quantum computer. Unlike a classical bit, which can only be 0 or 1, a qubit can exist in multiple states at the same time because of superposition.

Classical Bit

A classical computer stores information as:

  • 0
  • 1

Quantum Bit

A qubit can be represented as:

|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩

Here α and β are probability amplitudes that determine the likelihood of measuring 0 or 1.

Why are Qubits Powerful?

  • Can exist in superposition.
  • Can become entangled with other qubits.
  • Enable quantum algorithms to solve some problems more efficiently.
